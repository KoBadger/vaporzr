MIX IN FIVE MOVES

Everything here is one deck job at a time. No experience needed.

1 Load a song. Paste a name or link into Deck A and press LOAD.
2 Start it. Press PLAY. The green light means that deck is live.
3 Bring in the next. Load Deck B, press PLAY on it, then slide the fader toward B.
4 Keep them tight. Press SYNC and the two speeds match up.
5 Play around. Tap a pad to mark a moment, drag the platter to scrub, turn a filter left for deeper / right for brighter.
