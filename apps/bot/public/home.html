Everything a room needs

A queue everyone can share, a mixer for the host, and visuals for the wall.

🎛

Four-deck DJ mixer

Two banks, crossfader, EQ, filters, hot cues, loops, beat grid and per-deck pitch. C/D are line channels.

🌊

MilkDrop visuals

Butterchurn presets, beat reactivity and a live deck overlay you can control from the visualizer.

🎧

Lossless listening

Hear the bot’s 48 kHz stereo stream in a browser, ahead of Discord’s lossy relay.

📻

Any link with audio

Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Bandcamp, podcast feeds, video pages — the bot extracts the sound.

🎉

Jam requests

Scan a QR, pick a track, add your name. No app, no account, no voice-channel invite needed.

⏭

SponsorBlock

Skips sponsor and intro segments in YouTube/podcast audio automatically, so the room hears the track.
