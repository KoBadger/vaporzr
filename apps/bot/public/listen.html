Silence everywhere — press Start listening (browsers block audio until a tap), check the phone's volume, then hit Test tone. If the tone plays, this page is fine and the bot simply isn't playing.

Stutter or crackle — pick Safe, keep this tab in the foreground, and turn Boost off.

Only one side — turn Mono on (sums both channels into one).

Echo or double audio — mute Vaporzr in the Discord voice channel while listening here; otherwise you hear Discord's copy and this stream together.

Behind Discord — pick Instant to shave ~170 ms.
